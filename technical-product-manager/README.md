# Senior Technical Product Manager — AI & Developer Platform

Take-Home Challenge

Time: 5 days | Role: Senior Technical Product Manager

---

## Overview

This challenge evaluates your ability to think strategically about product in the intersection of AI, developer experience and enterprise software. You'll analyze a real product scenario, define a product strategy, write a PRD for a high-impact feature, and present your vision in a format that demonstrates product leadership, technical fluency and customer obsession.

This is not a coding challenge — it's a product leadership challenge. We're looking for someone who can deconstruct complex technical and business problems, make sharp prioritization decisions, and communicate a compelling product vision to both engineers and executives.

## Challenge Scenario

You've been hired as Voidr's first Senior Technical Product Manager. Here's where the product stands:

#### Current State

| Dimension | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **Product** | AI-powered test automation platform that deploys autonomous agents to test enterprise applications |
| **AI Agents** | Agents use Playwright + LLMs to autonomously explore and test web applications, finding bugs, regressions and UX issues |
| **Integrations** | GitHub Actions, GitLab CI (basic). Jenkins support requested by 3 enterprise accounts |
| **Customers** | 8 enterprise accounts (AB-InBev, Stone, Serasa, Philips, Valid, and 3 mid-market) |
| **Developer Adoption** | Developers at customer companies interact with Voidr mainly through CI/CD reports and a dashboard. No SDK, no API for custom workflows |
| **Top Customer Requests** | (1) API/SDK for custom test triggers, (2) Jenkins integration, (3) Agent configuration via code (IaC-style), (4) Better reporting & analytics for QA leads, (5) Slack/Teams notifications with AI-generated summaries |
| **Retention Signal** | NRR at 115%. Two accounts expanded after pilot, one account at risk due to "lack of developer adoption beyond QA team" |
| **Team** | 4 Software Engineers, 3 AI Test Engineers, 1 AI Deployment (CS), CTO leads product decisions |
| **Competitors** | Traditional test automation tools (Selenium, Cypress) evolving to add AI; AI-native startups emerging in exploratory testing |

#### The Strategic Question

How do you evolve Voidr from a **"managed AI testing service"** into a **"developer platform for AI-powered quality"** — where developers actively integrate, configure and extend Voidr's AI Agents into their workflows — while maintaining the white-glove enterprise value that drives current revenue?

## Challenge Components

### Part 1: Product Strategy Document (Written Deliverable)

Build a comprehensive product strategy document addressing the following:

| Section | What to Include |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Executive Summary** | One-page summary of your product vision, key bets, and expected outcomes for the next 12 months |
| **Market & Competitive Analysis** | Landscape of AI-powered testing, developer tools trends, and where Voidr's moat is (or should be). Who are the real competitors, and where is the white space? |
| **Customer Segmentation & Personas** | Who uses Voidr today vs. who should use it. Map personas (QA Lead, Staff Engineer, VP Engineering, DevOps Engineer) and their jobs-to-be-done |
| **Product Vision & Principles** | Articulate the north-star vision for Voidr's platform. Define 3-5 product principles that guide prioritization decisions |
| **Roadmap & Prioritization** | 12-month roadmap organized in themes/quarters. Show your prioritization framework — why these bets over others? Include what you're explicitly choosing NOT to do and why |
| **Developer Platform Strategy** | How you'd build the developer-facing layer: API, SDK, CLI, configuration-as-code, integrations ecosystem. How do you drive developer adoption without losing the enterprise buyer? |
| **Metrics & Success Criteria** | Define the product metrics framework: adoption, engagement, time-to-value, developer NPS, expansion signals. How do you measure if the platform strategy is working? |
| **Build vs. Buy vs. Partner** | For key capabilities (e.g., reporting/analytics, Slack integration, advanced CI/CD), when would you build in-house, use third-party tools, or partner? Defend your reasoning |

**Format:** Deliver as a PDF, Google Docs, or Notion document (10-15 pages recommended). Include diagrams, frameworks, or tables where they add clarity.

---

### Part 2: PRD — Developer SDK & API (Written Deliverable)

Write a Product Requirements Document (PRD) for the highest-impact feature from your roadmap: **Voidr's Developer SDK & API**.

This PRD should demonstrate your ability to go from strategy to execution. Include:

| Section | What to Include |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Problem Statement** | What problem does this solve? For whom? What's the cost of not solving it? |
| **Goals & Non-Goals** | What this initiative aims to achieve, and what it explicitly doesn't |
| **User Stories & Use Cases** | 3-5 detailed user stories from different personas (e.g., Staff Engineer triggering tests via API, DevOps Engineer configuring agents in YAML, QA Lead building custom reports) |
| **Proposed Solution** | High-level architecture of the SDK/API. What endpoints, what SDK methods, what configuration format. Include a code snippet showing how a developer would use it |
| **Technical Considerations** | Authentication model, rate limiting, versioning strategy, backward compatibility. You don't need to design the full system — show you think about the right things |
| **Success Metrics** | How you'd measure adoption and success of the SDK/API. What signals indicate it's working vs. failing? |
| **Rollout Plan** | How you'd ship this iteratively — alpha, beta, GA. How you'd get early feedback from design partners |
| **Risks & Mitigations** | What could go wrong and how you'd handle it |

**Format:** Deliver as part of the same document or as a separate file (5-8 pages recommended).

### Part 3: Product Vision Presentation (Video Deliverable)

Record a video (maximum **10 minutes**) presenting your product vision and roadmap to Voidr's leadership team.

#### Scenario

You're 45 days into the role. The founders have asked you to present your product assessment and 12-month roadmap to the leadership team. Attendees:

- **CTO / Co-founder** — Built the platform from zero. Deep expertise in AI Agents and Playwright. Cares about technical excellence and architecture decisions. Will challenge you on technical feasibility and sequencing.
- **CEO / Co-founder** — Focused on growth, customer retention and market positioning. Wants to understand how the product roadmap drives ARR and NRR. Will push back on anything that doesn't connect to revenue.
- **Head of AI Deployment (CS)** — Owns customer relationships. Hears feature requests daily. Will challenge you on customer prioritization and whether the roadmap addresses the at-risk account.

#### What You Must Demonstrate

1. **Product Vision**
   - Clear, compelling articulation of where Voidr's product should go
   - Why "developer platform" is the right bet (or argue for a different direction)
   - How this differentiates from competitors

2. **Prioritization Rigor**
   - Show your framework for deciding what to build and in what order
   - Defend at least one difficult trade-off (e.g., enterprise feature vs. developer platform investment)
   - Explain what you'd say "no" to and why

3. **Technical Fluency**
   - Demonstrate understanding of LLMs, AI Agents, CI/CD, developer workflows
   - Show you can have a productive conversation about architecture without being an engineer
   - Discuss technical feasibility considerations

4. **Customer-Centricity**
   - Show how customer insights drive your roadmap
   - Address the at-risk account scenario
   - Balance the needs of current enterprise customers with the platform vision

5. **Stakeholder Management**
   - Tailor your messaging for the different leaders in the room
   - Simulate handling pushback on at least 2 decisions
   - Show how you'd align engineering, sales and CS around the roadmap

#### Video Guidelines

- Record using Loom, OBS, or any screen recorder
- Use slides or a visual artifact (strongly recommended)
- Simulate stakeholder questions and show how you'd handle them
- Demonstrate the product leadership and communication skills expected at senior level

| **AI Usage** | Strategic and sophisticated use of AI tools throughout the process |

### What We're NOT Looking For

- Generic product frameworks: Show you understand Voidr's specific product, market and challenges
- Overloaded roadmaps: We value sharp prioritization over comprehensive feature lists
- Pixel-perfect mockups: Clarity of thinking matters more than visual design
- AI-generated content without refinement: The AI should accelerate your thinking, not replace it
- Enterprise-only or developer-only thinking: The challenge is balancing both

## About the Role

As Senior Technical Product Manager at Voidr, you'll own the product strategy for our AI-powered test automation platform and developer tools. You'll work at the intersection of AI, developer experience and enterprise software — defining how AI Agents evolve, how developers interact with the platform, and how the product drives growth.

Key responsibilities include:
- Defining the product roadmap and prioritizing features by impact
- Leading discovery with enterprise customers and translating insights into product decisions
- Shaping the developer experience (API, SDK, integrations, documentation)
- Collaborating with engineering on AI Agent capabilities and architecture
- Working with Sales and CS on packaging, pricing and customer expansion
