## Challenge Components

### Part 1: Business Case Document (Written Deliverable)

Create a comprehensive business case document to drive contract expansion with the following fictional account:

#### Target Account: TechLegacy ERP

**Company Profile:**
- Brazilian software house specializing in ERP systems for 25 years
- 800+ mid-to-large enterprise customers across Brazil and LATAM
- 150 employees, including 40 developers and 25 QA analysts
- Annual revenue: R$ 80 million
- Challenge: Each new customer deployment required 45 days of manual testing
- Market pressure: Competitors offering faster implementation cycles

**Implementation Results (After 4 weeks with Voidr):**

| Metric | Before | After | Impact |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| Testing time per deployment | 45 days | 32 minutes | 99.95% reduction |
| Test cases | ~50 manual scripts | 250 automated cases | 5x coverage |
| Coverage | Critical flows only | All main ERP flows | Comprehensive |
| Bugs found pre-production | ~15 per deployment | 47 per deployment | 3x more bugs caught |
| QA team utilization | 100% on regression | 20% on regression | 80% freed for exploratory |

**Current Contract:**
- Pilot covering 1 ERP module (Financial)
- 3-month engagement
- Investment: $45,000

**Expansion Opportunity:**
- 5 additional modules (Inventory, Sales, HR, Purchasing, Manufacturing)
- Rollout to customer deployment pipeline (avg. 40 deployments/year)

Your business case document must include:

| Section | What to Include |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Executive Summary** | One-page summary for CTO consumption with key metrics and recommendation |
| **Implementation Results Analysis** | Deep dive into the pilot results, what worked, lessons learned |
| **Business Impact Quantification** | Translation of technical metrics into business value (time saved, cost reduction, revenue impact) |
| **ROI Calculation** | Detailed ROI model with assumptions, payback period, and 3-year projection |
| **Expansion Proposal** | Phased approach for expanding to additional modules and deployment pipeline |
| **Risk Assessment** | Technical and business risks of expansion vs. risks of not expanding |
| **Competitive Positioning** | Why continuing with Voidr vs. alternatives (in-house, competitors) |
| **Investment & Timeline** | Proposed investment structure and implementation roadmap |

**Format:** Deliver as a PDF, Google Docs, or Notion document (10-15 pages recommended).

### Part 2: Executive Presentation (Video Deliverable)

Record a video (maximum **12 minutes**) delivering a Quarterly Business Review (QBR) presentation.

#### Scenario

You're presenting a QBR to TechLegacy's leadership after completing the 4-week pilot. This is your opportunity to showcase results and propose expansion. Attendees:

- **CTO** - Technical decision-maker, skeptical of AI hype, values concrete results
- **VP of Product** - Owns the ERP roadmap, concerned about implementation velocity
- **Head of Customer Success** - Responsible for customer deployments, directly impacted by testing bottlenecks

The pilot delivered:
- 250 AI-generated test cases covering the Financial module
- Testing time reduced from 45 days to 32 minutes
- 47 bugs caught pre-production (vs. typical 15 with manual testing)
- Zero critical bugs escaped to production during pilot period

#### What You Must Demonstrate

1. **Results Storytelling**
   - Present pilot outcomes in a compelling narrative
   - Connect technical metrics to business outcomes
   - Use data visualization effectively

2. **Business Value Translation**
   - Quantify time savings in business terms (cost, revenue, opportunity)
   - Calculate impact on customer satisfaction and deployment velocity
   - Project value at full scale across all modules

3. **Strategic Thinking**
   - Propose a clear expansion path
   - Address potential concerns proactively
   - Show understanding of their business context

4. **Stakeholder Management**
   - Tailor messaging for different stakeholders in the room
   - Handle a technical objection from the CTO (simulate)
   - Address budget concerns (simulate VP asking about investment)

5. **Expansion Ask**
   - Present clear proposal for next phase
   - Create urgency without being pushy
   - Define concrete next steps

#### Video Guidelines

- Record using Loom, OBS, or any screen recorder
- Use slides (strongly recommended for QBR format)
- Simulate stakeholder questions and show how you'd handle them
- Demonstrate the consultative approach expected in customer success
