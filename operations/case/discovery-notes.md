# Fictional CEO request

“People sometimes stay longer to finish a project. I want visibility, an explanation for the extra time and a way to offer a day off afterward. Some people are PJ. I also want all training and internal processes organized so new joiners don't depend on asking everyone.”

## What Operations Lead found

This fictional internal operation supports teams delivering work on critical customer applications. A missing access, unreliable record or lost follow-up can delay someone else's work. Operations Lead supervises you and retains policy and spending decisions. IT owns access provisioning; the hiring owner confirms interview arrangements; contract/legal owners review renewals. You coordinate the requests and maintain useful records.

You are not expected to wait for a complete briefing. Check the supplied evidence, identify a missing decision, prepare the next action and ask the responsible role for a specific answer. While waiting, choose other useful work that does not depend on that answer. Explain which alternative you recommend and its tradeoff.

- Different teams record time in messages and spreadsheets. There is no shared definition of eligible time, approver or correction process.
- One approval was requested twice; another request has a missing reason. One proposed day off conflicts with a critical handover.
- A PJ provider has an exceptional request with no confirmed contractual policy. An intern recorded time beyond their educational schedule.
- A manager said “approved” in conversation but the provided structured record still has no decision. Do not infer authorization.
- Training recordings and process documents have duplicate titles, absent owners, private links and obsolete instructions.
- We can start with a small pilot. No payroll integration, attendance surveillance or automatic policy rollout is requested in this exercise.

## Your decisions

Choose the intake fields, workflow, access roles, review routine and pilot scope. List decisions that require Operations Lead, the contract owner, relevant specialists or leadership. Provide a clear process for exceptions without pretending every uncertainty is solved.

All people, files and requests in this directory are fictional. Do not contact anyone.
