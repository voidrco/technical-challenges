Take-Home Challenge

## Overview

This challenge evaluates your ability to sell a technical product to technical buyers. As a Technical Account Executive at Voidr, you'll run the full enterprise sales cycle — from qualifying inbound leads (including AI-generated ones from our BDR Agent) to driving technical demos, designing PoCs, and closing deals with CTOs and VPs of Engineering.

You'll need to prove you can speak the language of engineering leaders, translate technical capabilities into business outcomes, and navigate the intersection of enterprise sales and developer tooling.

## Challenge Components

### Part 1: Technical Discovery & Deal Brief (Written Deliverable)

You've received an inbound lead from our BDR AI Agent. The prospect is a qualified contact who engaged with our outbound sequence. Your job is to run the discovery process and build a deal brief that sets up the next phase.

#### Target Account: StackPay

**Company Profile:**
- Brazilian payment infrastructure startup, Series B, valued at $800M
- 200+ engineers across 20 squads (core banking, checkout, anti-fraud, integrations)
- Processing 15 million transactions/day
- Stack: Node.js, Kotlin, AWS, Kubernetes
- CI/CD: GitHub Actions with ~45 minutes average pipeline time
- Current testing: unit tests + manual regression QA before major releases
- Pain: every release cycle includes a 3–5 day manual regression freeze; 2 production incidents in the last quarter caused by untested edge cases in the payment flow
- Expansion: launching a B2B embedded finance product in 2025, increasing release pressure

Your deal brief must include:

| Section | What to Include |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Technical Stakeholder Map** | Key decision-makers and influencers — create realistic personas (Head of Platform Engineering, VP of Engineering, QA Lead, CTO) with their technical concerns, motivations, and likely objections |
| **Technical Pain Analysis** | Map where their SDLC breaks down. What specifically causes the 3–5 day freeze? What would Voidr automate? Be specific about the technical problem, not just the business symptom |
| **MEDDIC Qualification Summary** | Fill out Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, and Champion for this account |
| **PoC Design** | Define the PoC scope: which squad, which application, what flows to automate, success criteria (with metrics), timeline (in weeks), and who owns what between Voidr and StackPay |
| **Technical Objection Handling** | Handle 3 technical objections you expect: (1) "We already use Cypress for E2E", (2) "We don't want another tool our devs have to maintain", (3) "Our flows are too complex for AI to test" |

**Format:** PDF, Google Docs, or Notion document (6–10 pages recommended).

### Part 2: Technical Demo + PoC Close (Video Deliverable)

Record a video (maximum **8 minutes**) simulating a technical closing meeting.

#### Scenario

You're in a meeting with StackPay's leadership 3 weeks after the PoC completed. Attendees:

- **VP of Engineering** — Your champion. Led the PoC with the anti-fraud squad. Technically sophisticated. Wants to roll this out to all 20 squads.
- **CTO** — Just joined the meeting. Skeptical about vendor lock-in. Wants to understand the architecture and maintenance model.
- **Head of Platform Engineering** — Worried about pipeline integration overhead and ownership of test failures.

The PoC showed:
- Regression freeze reduced from 5 days to 8 hours in the anti-fraud squad
- 9 critical edge cases detected before the last release
- 2 squads successfully integrated Voidr into their GitHub Actions pipelines

#### What You Must Demonstrate

1. **Technical Credibility**
   - Speak the language of the room — engineers, not MBA language
   - Show you understand their stack, their CI/CD pain, and how Voidr fits

2. **PoC Results Presentation**
   - Present the PoC outcomes using the metrics above
   - Extrapolate impact across all 20 squads
   - Translate technical wins into business outcomes (release velocity, incident reduction, engineering hours saved)

3. **Handling the CTO's Concerns**
   - Address the vendor lock-in concern with a clear, honest answer
   - Explain the architecture and what StackPay owns vs. what Voidr manages

4. **Close**
   - Propose the rollout plan (which squads, in what order, with what timeline)
   - Handle at least one objection live (the Head of Platform's concern about pipeline ownership)
   - Ask for the commitment to move forward

#### Video Guidelines

- Record using Loom, OBS, or any screen recorder
- You may use slides or speak directly to camera
- Simulate the room — you can voice the questions/objections yourself to show how you'd handle them
- The meeting should feel like a technical leadership room, not a board pitch

We use AI to run our own BDR and sales operations. We want to see how you use it as a force multiplier in the field.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Dimension | What We Evaluate |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Technical Fluency** | Ability to analyze a technical stack, identify SDLC bottlenecks, and propose technically credible solutions — without being an engineer |
| **Discovery Quality** | Depth of MEDDIC qualification, stakeholder mapping, and pain analysis |

| **AI Usage** | Strategic and practical use of AI to accelerate technical sales preparation |

### What We're NOT Looking For

- Engineer-level technical depth: you don't need to write code or architect solutions
- Generic SaaS sales playbooks: show you understand what makes Voidr technically differentiated
- A flawless video: we value clear thinking and authentic communication over polish
- AI-generated content without judgment: we want your analysis, your instincts, your experience
