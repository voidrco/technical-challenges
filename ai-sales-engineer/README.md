# AI Sales Engineer Technical Challenge

Take-Home Challenge

Time: 5 days | Role: AI Sales Engineer · Internship / Early Career

---

## Overview

This challenge evaluates how you learn about an account, use AI to prepare a relevant approach, start a commercial conversation, and keep the next action organized.

You will receive a research packet about a fictional enterprise software company. Your job is to separate signals from assumptions, choose an executive and a potential technical champion, prepare personalized outreach, simulate a cold call, and show how an AI SDR, Lemlist and a CRM would support the work.

We evaluate your decisions, learning and execution. Previous sales employment and a paid tool subscription are not required. Bring an explainable programming project from study or personal work; professional development experience is not required. The exercise itself focuses on commercial execution.

> **Language requirement:** Submit the HTML and record the presentation in **English**. AI-assisted writing and language preparation are welcome. We assess understandable communication and your ability to explain your decisions, not accent or native fluency.

## Voidr Engagement Model — Fixed Premises

Use these premises for the exercise:

- **Start with an observed signal:** a company initiative may justify research; it does not prove a problem, budget or willingness to buy.
- **Approach two distinct roles:** an executive who may sponsor the conversation and a technical person who may help investigate. A job title alone does not establish sponsorship or champion status.
- **Earn a relevant next conversation:** propose a short discussion about one operational hypothesis and what would make it worth investigating.
- **Qualify before committing:** ask about priority, impact, the people involved and a feasible next step. Do not promise pricing, a free engagement, delivery dates or technical feasibility.
- **Separate commercial and delivery ownership:** the sales team validates commercial commitments; AI Deployment and engineering assess diagnosis, scope and implementation.
- **Use AI with review:** AI can research, draft and propose actions; you remain responsible for checking sources, choosing recipients and reviewing messages.
- **Keep the record useful:** every contact has an observed outcome, an owner and a next action. Stop the sequence when the contact replies, declines or asks not to be contacted.
- **Simulation only:** do not contact real people, place real cold calls, enroll real leads or launch campaigns. Use only the fictional packet and dummy records.

Your job is to turn these premises into a small, executable prospecting workflow. A meeting booked is not, by itself, a qualified opportunity.

## Your Mission

Prepare an account approach for **Northstar Retail Systems**.

Your work should help the commercial team:

1. understand why this account might be worth approaching now, and what remains unknown;
2. choose one executive and one potential technical champion, with a distinct reason to contact each;
3. review a short, personalized approach and a small cadence before anything is sent;
4. see you start a cold call, listen to objections and qualify a next conversation;
5. inspect how the AI SDR, campaign settings and CRM record prevent unsupported claims, duplicate outreach and missed follow-ups.

Focus on one account and one problem hypothesis. You are not expected to design an implementation, run a PoC or close a contract.

## Challenge Components

### Part 1: Account Approach Deck

Create a concise internal working presentation using the provided HTML template.

Your deck should demonstrate:

- a researched account thesis supported by packet IDs, plus the two official Voidr sources;
- evidence, hypotheses and missing information visibly separated;
- a reason to approach the executive and potential technical champion differently;
- **two initial emails**, one per persona (up to 100 words each), **one LinkedIn connection note** (up to 200 characters), and **one follow-up** that adds a useful fact or question;
- a **four-step cadence** for each persona: channel, day, purpose and stop rule; the initial email, LinkedIn touch, simulated call and follow-up can be reused across these steps;
- an AI SDR instruction with inputs, allowed sources, expected output, human review and stop conditions; show **two short dry runs**, including one where the agent must refuse to invent missing evidence or stop contact;
- a Lemlist-style setup table with dummy recipient, custom variables, channel, delay, paused state and reply/opt-out handling; flag missing contact data rather than fabricating an address;
- a CRM record for each selected persona, with source, last interaction, status, owner and next action; update one after the simulated call.

Use compact tables or an appendix inside the same HTML. You do not need to build an integration, write code or open a paid tool account. A correct simulation is sufficient.

#### Format and Design Requirements

- **4–6 slides** recommended; an appendix inside the same HTML is allowed for the operational tables
- Use the provided [standalone HTML template](./template/voidr-sales-template.html)
- You may remove, duplicate, reorder or adapt the layouts
- Preserve recognizable Voidr branding and legible hierarchy
- Submit a **single self-contained `.html` file** that opens directly in a modern browser
- All visible presentation copy must be in **English**
- Replace all bracketed placeholder copy and visible `Template · ...` instructions before submission

You may use AI to edit the HTML. Front-end engineering sophistication is not an evaluation criterion. Clear research, usable outreach and sound operating decisions are.

### Part 2: Sales Conversation and Workflow (Video Deliverable)

Record a video between **10 and 15 minutes** showing your account approach and simulating the conversation.

#### Scenario

You are preparing your first approach to the fictional account. The roles you will encounter are:

- **Commercial Reviewer** — Wants to understand the signal, the chosen personas and why the message is relevant
- **Potential Technical Champion** — Says the team already has automation and questions the need for another vendor
- **Executive Prospect** — Asks why this matters now and whether you can support the claimed business value
- **Executive Gatekeeper** — Protects the executive's time and asks you to state a relevant reason for a meeting

#### What You Must Demonstrate

1. **Account Research**
   - Explain the signal, sources, hypothesis and unknowns
   - Justify the two personas without assuming authority, interest or an existing relationship
   - Show one AI-generated suggestion you corrected because the source did not support it

2. **Personalized Outreach**
   - Present the two emails and explain the difference in each ask
   - Explain the cadence, contact-data gaps and checks required before launch
   - Avoid invented familiarity, ROI, customer claims or artificial urgency

3. **AI SDR and Commercial Operations**
   - Show your instruction and two dry runs
   - Walk through the paused Lemlist-style setup and the CRM records
   - Explain what requires human approval and how a reply or opt-out changes the next step

4. **Objection Handling**
   - Simulate a **2–3 minute cold call** within the video, including opening, permission to continue, one qualification question and a next-step ask
   - Simulate and answer at least **two prospect objections** and **one executive gatekeeper objection** from the packet
   - You may play both sides. When the prospect corrects your hypothesis, change your approach; do not simply read a pitch

5. **Next Steps**
   - State whether the call justified a next conversation, more research or stopping
   - Update the CRM record with the actual simulated outcome, owner and next action
   - Give the next person enough context to continue without repeating the research

#### Video Guidelines

- Use Loom, OBS or any screen recorder
- Show the HTML, including the operational tables; clearly label internal planning and prospect-facing copy
- Simulate questions and objections yourself; no second participant is needed
- Keep the complete recording between **10 and 15 minutes**; submissions longer than 15 minutes may not be reviewed in full
- All spoken and visible content must be in **English**; notes and rehearsal with AI are allowed
- Never contact real prospects or send any of the prepared messages
