# AI Deployment Technical Challenge

Take-Home Challenge

Time: 5 days | Role: AI Deployment Senior

---

## Overview

This challenge evaluates the work an AI Deployment professional performs before and inside a consequential customer meeting.

You will receive notes from an initial discovery call with a fictional enterprise software company. Your job is to decide what matters, build a preliminary diagnosis, frame the most relevant Voidr capabilities, propose a measurable next step, and present your recommendation to a customer and an executive gatekeeper.

The source material is intentionally incomplete. We are evaluating your judgment, not your ability to manufacture certainty.

> **Language requirement:** All submitted materials and the recorded presentation must be in **English**.

## Voidr Engagement Model — Fixed Premises

Use the following premises as given. You are not expected to invent a commercial model for the initial engagement.

- **No setup fee or upfront investment:** the initial diagnostic and value-demonstration phase does not require an initial financial commitment from the customer.
- **Start with the bottleneck:** Voidr first identifies the operational constraint and agrees on the business or delivery KPI that matters.
- **One week to establish the baseline:** Voidr uses available evidence to understand the current workflow, quantify the starting point, and identify the highest-value opportunity.
- **Two weeks to demonstrate value:** Voidr proposes a focused scope and shows how the solution works against a real customer journey or problem.
- **Low customer effort:** Voidr leads the technical work through a senior AI Deployment / Forward Deployed Engineer. The customer provides the minimum access, context, and validation required.
- **Integration work is part of the scope:** when a standard connector does not exist, Voidr can design the necessary custom connector as part of the engagement.
- **Enable the internal team:** the objective is not to create another dependency. Voidr implements the solution and prepares the customer's team to operate and expand it.
- **Continue only when value is demonstrated:** if the initial work proves meaningful value, Voidr and the customer define a tailored partnership. If it does not, the engagement stops without a broader commitment.

Your job is to apply this model intelligently to the case: choose the right bottleneck, baseline, first scope, success signal, customer inputs, and next decision.

## Your Mission

Prepare a customer-facing preliminary diagnosis for **Northstar Retail Systems**.

The meeting should help the customer:

1. confirm or correct your understanding of the current situation;
2. see the prioritized problems and their operational consequences;
3. understand which Voidr capabilities may fit — and which assumptions still need validation;
4. evaluate a focused initial value-demonstration hypothesis;
5. agree on a concrete next step, with people, inputs, outputs, and a decision rule.

Your recommendation does not need to include every Voidr capability. Good scope judgment is more valuable than breadth.

## Challenge Components

### Part 1: Customer Diagnostic Deck

Create a concise customer-facing presentation using the provided HTML template.

Your deck should demonstrate:

- a clear reading of the customer's context and why it matters now;
- a prioritized diagnosis rather than a restatement of every discovery note;
- a defensible causal chain from technical conditions to team workload, release risk, customer impact, and business relevance;
- visible separation between evidence, interpretation, and open questions;
- solution framing tied to specific problems, including boundaries and dependencies;
- a focused initial value-demonstration hypothesis with baseline, measurement, ownership, risks, and a go/no-go rule;
- a concrete next-step ask.

#### Format and Design Requirements

- **4–6 slides** recommended
- Use the provided [standalone HTML template](./template/voidr-diagnostic-template.html)
- You may remove, duplicate, reorder, or adapt the layouts
- Preserve recognizable Voidr branding and the editorial quality of the template
- Submit a **single self-contained `.html` file** that opens directly in a modern browser
- All visible presentation copy must be in **English**
- Remove all bracketed placeholder copy and every visible `Template · ...` instruction before submission

You may use AI to edit the HTML. Front-end engineering sophistication is not an evaluation criterion; clarity, hierarchy, and customer communication are.

---

### Part 2: Customer Diagnostic Presentation (Video Deliverable)

Record a video between **10 and 15 minutes** presenting your preliminary diagnosis to the fictional customer.

#### Scenario

You are leading the follow-up meeting after the initial discovery call. The customer participants are:

- **Customer Sponsor** — Focused on customer trust, operational friction, and taking a defensible recommendation to the executive committee
- **Principal Engineer** — Skeptical of generic AI claims and concerned about legacy feasibility, internal-tool overlap, and integration effort
- **Quality & Support Lead** — Focused on manual workload, adoption, customer escalations, and practical operability
- **Executive Gatekeeper** — Challenging business value, customer effort, timing, risk, and the case for investment

#### What You Must Demonstrate

1. **Preliminary Diagnosis**
   - Explain the most important evidence, your interpretation, and the critical unknowns
   - Prioritize the underlying problems instead of repeating every discovery note
   - Connect technical conditions to workload, release risk, customer impact, and business relevance

2. **Solution Framing**
   - Map the relevant Voidr capabilities to the prioritized problems
   - Explain important boundaries, dependencies, and what you would not recommend yet
   - Differentiate the proposal from generic AI code review or an internal automation initiative

3. **Initial Value Demonstration**
   - Apply the fixed Voidr engagement model described above
   - Define the one-week baseline, the focused two-week demonstration, the customer inputs, and Voidr's responsibilities
   - State the success signal and the decision rule for continuing, adjusting, or stopping

4. **Objection Handling**
   - Simulate and answer at least **two customer objections** and **one executive gatekeeper objection**
   - Cover technical fit, customer effort, build-vs-buy, value, risk, or commercial justification
   - Answer honestly when the evidence is insufficient; do not manufacture certainty

5. **Next Steps**
   - Close with a specific, low-friction next step
   - Make ownership, required inputs, immediate output, and the next decision explicit

#### Video Guidelines

- Record using Loom, OBS, or any screen recorder
- Present the provided HTML deck as if the customer were in the room
- Simulate the questions and objections yourself to demonstrate how you would respond
- Keep the complete presentation between **10 and 15 minutes**; submissions longer than 15 minutes may not be reviewed in full
- All spoken and visible presentation content must be in **English**
- The meeting should feel like a concise customer conversation, not a generic company pitch

---

### Part 3: AI-Assisted Preparation (Required)

You **must** use AI tools during this challenge. AI is part of the role, not an optional shortcut.

Use AI where it improves your work — for example, to organize evidence, test competing diagnoses, research Voidr, challenge scope, prepare objections, or edit the HTML presentation.

You do not need to include an AI explanation in the customer-facing video. Keep a record of the tools, prompts, outputs, corrections, and rejected suggestions that materially influenced your work. If you pass this stage, you will discuss your AI usage with the founders.

Do not include confidential information from current or former employers.
