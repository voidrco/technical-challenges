# Chief Revenue Officer Technical Challenge

Take-Home Challenge

Time: 7 days | Role: Chief Revenue Officer

---

## Overview

This challenge evaluates your ability to think at the strategic level required to build and scale a revenue engine from the ground up. You'll develop a comprehensive Go-to-Market and revenue growth strategy for Voidr, and present it in a board-level format that demonstrates executive leadership, financial rigor, and commercial vision.

This is not a sales challenge — it's a leadership challenge. We're looking for someone who can architect the entire revenue function (Sales, Customer Success, Marketing), define the operating model, and defend it under investor-grade scrutiny.

## Challenge Scenario

You've been hired as Voidr's first Chief Revenue Officer. Here's where the company stands:

#### Current State

| Dimension | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **ARR** | $1.5M |
| **Customers** | 8 enterprise accounts (AB-InBev, Stone, Serasa, Philips, Valid, and 3 mid-market) |
| **ACV** | Average $185K, ranging from $80K to $350K |
| **Sales Cycle** | 3-6 months for enterprise, 4-8 weeks for mid-market |
| **Team** | 2 BDRs, 1 Account Executive, 1 AI Deployment (CS), no dedicated Marketing |
| **Churn** | 0% logo churn (too early to tell), 1 account at risk of downsizing |
| **Pipeline** | $800K in qualified pipeline, 60% from founder-led sales |
| **Funding** | Post-Series A, 18 months of runway |
| **Product** | AI-powered test automation platform with strong product-market fit in mission-critical systems |

#### The Board's Expectation

Scale from **$1.5M to $10M ARR in 18 months** while building a repeatable, scalable revenue engine that reduces dependency on founder-led sales.

## Challenge Components

### Part 1: Revenue Strategy (Written Deliverable)

Build a comprehensive revenue strategy document addressing the following:

| Section | What to Include |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Executive Summary** | One-page summary of your strategy, key bets, and expected outcomes |
| **Market Analysis & Segmentation** | Target segments, ICP definition, TAM/SAM/SOM, and why you'd prioritize certain segments over others |
| **Go-to-Market Architecture** | Channel strategy, sales motions (enterprise vs. mid-market), partnership opportunities, and inbound/outbound mix |
| **Pricing & Packaging** | Pricing architecture proposal — how you'd structure pricing to maximize ACV, enable land-and-expand, and compete effectively |
| **Revenue Model & Forecast** | 18-month financial model with monthly/quarterly projections, assumptions, and scenario analysis (base, bull, bear) |
| **Unit Economics** | CAC, LTV, payback period, magic number — current state analysis and target state |
| **Org Design & Hiring Plan** | Team structure for Sales, CS, and Marketing. Who you'd hire first, second, third. Rationale for sequencing and expected ramp times |
| **Operational Playbooks** | Key processes you'd build in the first 90 days: pipeline management, forecasting cadence, handoff protocols, QBR structure |
| **Retention & Expansion** | NRR strategy — how you'd achieve 120%+ NRR through upsell, cross-sell, and expansion motions |
| **Risk Analysis** | Top 5 risks to the plan and specific mitigation strategies for each |

**Format:** Deliver as a PDF, Google Docs, or Notion document (12-18 pages recommended). Include charts, tables, or models where they add clarity.

---

### Part 2: Board Presentation (Video Deliverable)

Record a video (maximum **15 minutes**) presenting your revenue strategy to Voidr's board.

#### Scenario

You're 30 days into the CRO role. The board has asked you to present your assessment of the current revenue operation and your 18-month plan. Attendees:

- **CEO / Co-founder** — Built the company from zero, led sales personally until now. Wants to ensure the transition from founder-led sales is handled correctly. Deep technical background.
- **Lead Investor (Series A)** — Focused on capital efficiency, path to Series B metrics, and whether the team can execute. Will challenge your assumptions on hiring velocity and CAC payback.
- **Independent Board Member** — Former CRO of a $100M+ ARR SaaS company. Will probe your strategic thinking and operational rigor. Expects defensible numbers.

#### What You Must Demonstrate

1. **Strategic Vision**
   - Clear thesis on how Voidr gets from $1.5M to $10M
   - Prioritization framework for market segments and sales motions
   - Understanding of what makes this company and market unique

2. **Financial Rigor**
   - Revenue model with defensible assumptions
   - Unit economics analysis and improvement plan
   - Hiring plan tied to revenue capacity model

3. **Operational Depth**
   - First 90-day plan with concrete milestones
   - Process and tooling decisions with rationale
   - Metrics framework and reporting cadence

4. **Leadership Presence**
   - Confidence and credibility appropriate for C-level / board context
   - Ability to defend decisions under pressure
   - Simulate handling pushback on at least 2 assumptions (e.g., hiring timeline, CAC projections, or segment prioritization)

5. **Transition Strategy**
   - How you'd transition from founder-led sales without losing momentum
   - Knowledge transfer and relationship handoff plan

#### Video Guidelines

- Record using Loom, OBS, or any screen recorder
- Use slides (strongly recommended for board format)
- Simulate board member questions — show how you'd handle their challenges
- Demonstrate the executive presence and strategic depth expected at C-level

## Evaluation Criteria

| Dimension | What We Evaluate |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Strategic Thinking** | Quality of market analysis, segmentation, and GTM architecture. Ability to make and defend prioritization decisions. |
| **Financial Acumen** | Rigor of revenue model, unit economics analysis, and scenario planning. Are the numbers defensible? |
| **Operational Excellence** | Depth of operational playbooks, process design, and metrics framework. Can you build the machine, not just the strategy? |
| **Leadership & Presence** | Executive presence in board setting, ability to handle pushback, confidence in defending decisions |
| **Organizational Design** | Quality of hiring plan, team structure rationale, and understanding of how to build and scale revenue teams |
| **AI Usage** | Strategic and sophisticated use of AI tools throughout the process |

### What We're NOT Looking For

- Generic GTM frameworks: Show you understand Voidr's specific market, customers, and challenges
- Unrealistic projections: We value grounded, defensible assumptions over optimistic hockey sticks
- MBA-template strategies: We want to see your judgment and pattern recognition from real experience
- AI-generated content without refinement: The AI should accelerate your thinking, not replace it
- Over-polished decks: Substance and clarity beat design
